2. Key EDA Findings

  • Data Cleaning: 11 rows had whitespace in TotalCharges (all with tenure == 0), safely imputed with 0.0. Zero duplicate rows.
  • Contract Type: Month-to-month contracts churn at ~42.7% vs ~2.8% for 2-year contracts.
  • Tenure: New customers (0–12 months) carry the highest churn risk.
  • Payment Method: Electronic check users churn at ~45.3% vs ~15–17% for automatic payments.
